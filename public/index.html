How to play

Every day there's a new mystery talent for each mode. You get 10 guesses. Find them in as few as you can!

Classic

Type any talent's name. Each trait of your guess is compared with today's talent:

  • Green: exact match
  • Orange: partly right (debuted the same year, or born the same month)
  • Red: no match
  • Arrow: today's talent is taller, or debuted later (▲) / shorter or earlier (▼)

Hints unlock after a few guesses if you get stuck.

Portrait

Guess who's in a zoomed-in, black-and-white picture that's turned the wrong way. Each wrong guess zooms out and sharpens it, then the color comes back, and finally it turns upright.

Birthdays

Drag 5 talents into birthday order, January on the left. You get 3 tries (the 3 dots), and anyone in the right spot locks in place after each check.

Your progress and stats are saved in this browser.
